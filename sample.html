    Our Mission

    Waste → Value

    CARES drives a waste-to-value transformation in Ethiopia's coffee sector — closing the circular loop for smallholder farmers, cooperatives, and the environment.

    Research Framework

    Research Pillars

    Pillar 1

    Specialty Coffee

    Premium Valorization methods like Anaerobic Fermentation and Artisanal Coffee Wine.

    Learn More
    Pillar 2

    Agro-Energy & Earth Care

    Transforming coffee waste into Sustainable Bioenergy (Biogas) and Regenerative Biocompost.

    Bio-Extracted Innovation

    Developing Botanical Pesticides and High-Value Cosmetics from coffee cherry natural defenses.
